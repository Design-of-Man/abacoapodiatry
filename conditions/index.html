What We Treat

Foot & Ankle Conditions We Treat

If it happens below the knee, we've treated it. Explore the conditions below to understand your symptoms, your options, and how laser and regenerative therapy fit in — or skip the reading and let Dr. Cedeno figure it out with you.

Plantar Fasciitis

The #1 cause of heel pain — that stabbing first-step pain in the morning. Exceptionally responsive to laser and shockwave therapy, even in chronic cases that failed injections.

Learn more

Heel Pain & Heel Spurs

Fasciitis is common, but it isn't the only cause. Spurs, bursitis, nerve entrapment, fat pad atrophy, and stress fractures all masquerade as "heel pain" — and each needs different treatment.

Learn more

Peripheral Neuropathy

Burning, tingling, numbness, or that "walking on socks" feeling. Drug-free laser therapy offers a different path than a lifetime of gabapentin.

Learn more

Achilles Tendonitis

Pain and stiffness in the cord above your heel. Catch it early and heal it — chronic Achilles degeneration risks rupture and responds slower.

Learn more

Foot & Ankle Arthritis

33 joints in each foot, and arthritis can strike any of them. Reduce inflammation and stay active without living on pills.

Learn more

Sports Injuries

Runners, golfers, tennis and pickleball players: heal faster and return to play stronger with accelerated-recovery protocols.

Learn more

Ankle Sprains & Strains

"Just a sprain" is how chronic ankle instability starts. Proper treatment now prevents years of re-injury later.

Learn more

Diabetic Foot Care

Diabetes makes every foot problem more serious. Specialist care for neuropathy, circulation, skin and nail issues — and prevention that protects you long-term.

Learn more
