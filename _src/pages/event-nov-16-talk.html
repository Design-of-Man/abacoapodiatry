FAQ

Before you come

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no cost to attend, nothing is sold at the talk, and no insurance is involved. Light bites and drinks are on us.

Do I need to be a patient?

No. Current patients, their family members, and anyone curious are welcome. Bring a friend if they'd get something out of it; just choose "Me plus one guest" when you RSVP so we have a seat for them.

Will I be examined?

No. It's a talk and a conversation, not an appointment. If your question needs a proper look, we can set up a visit for another day before you leave.

Where do I park?

Free parking on site at 4601 Military Trail. We're in Suite 202. Maps and directions.

What if I can't make it after all?

Please call (561) 915-1934 so we can give your seat to someone else. Seating is limited.

This talk is for general education. It isn't a medical appointment, and nothing said there replaces an exam, diagnosis, or treatment plan from your own provider.
