What is MLS laser therapy?

MLS (Multiwave Locked System) laser therapy is an FDA-cleared treatment that uses two synchronized wavelengths of laser light to reduce pain and inflammation and speed up your body's natural healing. Unlike medication, which masks symptoms, laser energy works at the cellular level, stimulating the mitochondria in damaged cells to produce more energy, repair faster, and clear inflammation.

The "locked" in Multiwave Locked System is what sets MLS apart from ordinary cold lasers: its two wavelengths fire in a patented, synchronized pulse. The 808 nm continuous wave penetrates deep to resolve inflammation and swelling, while the 905 nm pulsed wave acts on nerve conduction to relieve pain. Delivered together, their combined effect is stronger than either delivered alone.

At Abacoa Podiatry & Leg Vein Center, every laser treatment plan is prescribed and supervised by Dr. Orlando Cedeno, DPM, a board-certified foot and ankle surgeon, so your laser therapy is part of a real medical diagnosis and plan, not a one-size-fits-all wellness service.

Mechanism

What the laser actually does inside your tissue

Photobiomodulation is not heat and it's not magic. It's well-studied cell biology.

1. Cellular energy production

Light photons are absorbed by cytochrome c oxidase in your cells' mitochondria, boosting ATP production, the fuel cells use to repair themselves. Damaged tissue literally gets more energy to heal.

2. Inflammation & swelling resolve

Laser energy improves microcirculation and lymphatic drainage while modulating inflammatory mediators. Swelling drains, stiffness eases, and the inflammatory cycle that keeps injuries stuck finally breaks.

3. Pain signals quiet down

The pulsed 905 nm wavelength acts on nerve fibers to slow pain signal transmission and trigger endorphin release. Fast relief you can feel, without a single pill.

Your Visit

What to expect at your laser session

  1. Check in and get comfortable

    No gowns, no prep. You'll sit back in a treatment chair and simply expose the foot or ankle being treated.

  2. The robotic laser goes to work

    Our MLS system's robotic head positions itself over your treatment area and delivers the synchronized laser dose with computer-controlled precision. You'll feel little or nothing, many patients read or answer email during treatment.

  3. Done in 10–15 minutes

    There's no recovery period. Drive, work, walk the dog, your day continues exactly as planned.

  4. Feel the cumulative effect

    Relief often begins within the first 1–3 sessions and builds with each visit, because the biological effects of each treatment compound. Most plans run 6–12 sessions, 2–3 times per week.

FAQ

MLS laser therapy questions, answered

Does MLS laser therapy hurt?

No. Treatment is completely painless, most patients feel nothing, or a mild pleasant warmth. There are no needles, incisions, or numbing required.

How fast will I feel results?

Many patients notice reduced pain within their first 1–3 sessions. Because effects are cumulative, improvement typically continues throughout the treatment course, and even for weeks after it ends, as tissue continues rebuilding.

Is it safe? Are there side effects?

MLS laser therapy is FDA-cleared and has an excellent safety record, with no significant side effects reported in clinical use. Standard precautions apply: we don't treat over active cancer, over a pregnant uterus, or near the eyes. Dr. Cedeno screens every patient before starting.

How is MLS different from "cold laser" (LLLT)?

Traditional cold lasers use a single low-power wavelength, which limits depth and dose. MLS is a higher-powered Class IV system that synchronizes two wavelengths in a patented pulse, delivering a therapeutic dose to deep tissue in far less time, one reason sessions take only 10–15 minutes.

Does insurance cover laser therapy?

Usually laser therapy itself is an out-of-pocket service, while your evaluation and related podiatric care are often covered. We provide clear package pricing before you start, call (561) 915-1934 and we'll verify your benefits.

Can laser therapy be combined with other treatments?

Yes, and it often should be. Laser pairs beautifully with shockwave therapy, PRP, orthotics, and stretching programs. Dr. Cedeno designs combination plans when they'll get you better faster.
