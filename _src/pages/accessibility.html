Accessibility Statement

Everyone deserves equal access to health information. We built this site with that in mind.

Our commitment

Abacoa Podiatry & Leg Vein Center is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including visitors who rely on screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive technologies. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we've built in

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper headings and landmarks
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, menus, and interactive elements
  • A visible "skip to main content" link
  • Sufficient color contrast and readable, scalable text
  • Descriptive link text and alternative text for meaningful images
  • Reduced-motion support. Animations are disabled for visitors whose devices request it

Room to improve? Tell us.

If any part of this site is difficult for you to use, we genuinely want to know. Call us at (561) 624-4800 or use our contact page, and we'll both help you get the information you need and work to fix the issue.

In our office

Our physical office is wheelchair accessible with free on-site parking. If you have specific accessibility needs for your visit, let us know when scheduling and we'll be ready for you.
