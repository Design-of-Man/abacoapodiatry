Symptom Guide

Where does it hurt?

Find the spot that matches your pain to see what's most commonly behind it, and how we treat it.

Heel

Stabbing heel pain, especially first steps?

Worst on your first steps of the morning? That is the signature of plantar fasciitis. Heel spurs and nerve entrapment can also be behind it.

  • Plantar fasciitis · Heel pain overview
Get It Evaluated
Arch

Aching or tearing pain through the arch?

Usually plantar fasciitis, posterior tibial tendon strain, or fallen arches. Untreated, it changes how you walk and sends pain up to knees, hips and back.

  • Plantar fasciitis · Strains
Get It Evaluated
Toes & Ball of Foot

Burning, tingling, or numbness in your toes?

Burning or pins and needles in the toes is a hallmark of peripheral neuropathy, especially with diabetes. A stiff, sore big toe joint usually signals arthritis.

  • Neuropathy · Arthritis · Diabetic foot care
Get It Evaluated
Achilles & Back of Ankle

Pain or stiffness above your heel?

Pain along the cord behind your ankle is usually Achilles tendonitis, an overuse injury. Chronic cases build scar tissue that responds well to laser and shockwave.

  • Achilles tendonitis · Sports injuries
Get It Evaluated
Ankle

Swollen, unstable, or aching ankle?

Pain after a twist is a sprain, and sprains that never fully healed are a leading cause of chronic instability. Deep aching often means ankle arthritis.

  • Sprains & strains · Ankle arthritis
Get It Evaluated
Top of Foot

Soreness across the top of your foot?

Often extensor tendonitis, a stress reaction from overuse, or midfoot arthritis. Usually athletes, or anyone who ramped up activity quickly.

  • Sports injuries · Arthritis
Get It Evaluated

What laser treatment actually looks like

MLS laser therapy delivers two synchronized wavelengths deep into injured tissue, triggering your own cells to calm inflammation and rebuild. No heat damage, no needles, no recovery time.

How MLS Laser Works See our technology

  1. Evaluation & diagnosis

    We examine your foot and ankle, review imaging if needed, and pinpoint the true pain generator.

Good Questions

Quick answers before you call

Does laser therapy hurt?

No. Most patients feel nothing at all, or a mild warmth. No needles, no incisions, no recovery time. You walk in, relax for 10–15 minutes, and walk out.

How many sessions will I need?

Most conditions respond within 6–12 sessions, 2–3 times per week. Acute injuries need fewer, long-standing ones more. Dr. Cedeno sets your plan after the evaluation and adjusts as you improve.

Is MLS laser therapy FDA cleared?

Yes, FDA-cleared to treat pain and inflammation and to promote healing. It is used in thousands of clinics worldwide with no significant reported side effects.

Will insurance cover my treatment?

Your evaluation and most podiatric care are typically covered. Laser and regenerative therapies are usually elective, and we quote exact pricing up front. Call (561) 915-1934 and we’ll verify your benefits first.
