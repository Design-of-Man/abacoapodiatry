Treatments That Heal — Not Just Numb

Every service we offer shares one philosophy: stimulate the body's own repair systems first, reserve surgery for when it's truly needed. Explore each treatment below, or let Dr. Cedeno match the right one to your condition.

Book an Evaluation (561) 624-4800

MLS Laser Therapy

Our flagship service. FDA-cleared dual-wavelength laser reduces pain and inflammation while accelerating tissue repair. Painless 10–15 minute sessions, zero downtime, and results many patients feel within the first few visits. The go-to treatment for plantar fasciitis, neuropathy, tendonitis, and arthritis.

MLS laser in depth

Shockwave Therapy (EPAT)

Focused acoustic pressure waves break down chronic scar tissue, stimulate new blood vessel growth, and restart healing in tissue that's given up. Especially powerful for stubborn plantar fasciitis and chronic Achilles tendinopathy that has resisted everything else.

Shockwave in depth

Stem Cell Therapy

Regenerative cell treatments deliver powerful biologic material to damaged tendons, ligaments, and joints, supporting genuine tissue repair rather than temporary relief. An option worth understanding before you agree to major surgery.

Stem cell therapy in depth

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP)

Your own blood, concentrated into a growth-factor-rich serum and precisely injected where healing has stalled. Natural, well-researched, and highly effective for chronic tendon and fascia injuries.

PRP in depth

Regenerative Medicine Programs

How it all fits together. Dr. Cedeno combines laser, shockwave, PRP, and cell therapy into staged healing programs matched to your condition, timeline, and goals — with clear milestones along the way.
