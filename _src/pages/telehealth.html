Care From Home

Telehealth Visits

Some visits need hands on your foot. Plenty don't. For follow-ups, results reviews, wound-photo checks, and "should I come in?" questions, a video visit saves you the drive.

Call to Schedule Telehealth

Good candidates for a virtual visit

  • Follow-up discussions of imaging or lab results
  • Progress checks during a treatment program
  • Medication and post-procedure questions
  • Visual skin, nail, and wound checks (with photos)
  • Deciding whether a new symptom needs an in-person exam

What still needs an office visit

Diagnosis of a new pain problem, laser and shockwave treatments themselves, injections, imaging, and anything requiring hands-on examination. Telehealth complements your care here. It doesn't replace the exam room.

How it works

  1. Call to schedule

    Ring (561) 624-4800 and ask about a telehealth appointment. Our team confirms whether your concern fits a virtual format.

  2. Get your link

    We'll send instructions for joining your video visit, a smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera is all you need.

  3. Meet with us

    Same unhurried conversation you'd get in the office, from your couch.
