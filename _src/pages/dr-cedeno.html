Training built for hard problems

Dr. Cedeno's path to podiatric surgery began with science: a bachelor's degree in chemistry from the University of Pittsburgh, followed by his medical degree from the Barry University School of Podiatric Medicine & Surgery in Miami, Florida.

He then completed a three-year surgical residency in reconstructive and trauma surgery of the foot and ankle at Chestnut Hill Hospital / University of Pennsylvania in Philadelphia, the kind of training where complex fractures, deformity corrections, and limb-threatening diabetic complications are the daily curriculum. He has particular expertise in the conservative and surgical treatment of diabetic conditions affecting the lower extremities.

In practice at Abacoa Podiatry & Leg Vein Center, Dr. Cedeno saw the same story repeat: patients arriving after months of temporary fixes, their conditions now chronic. It's what drove him to build Jupiter Laser & Regenerative Medicine, bringing MLS laser therapy, shockwave, and regenerative treatments to Jupiter so patients could access genuine healing technologies without leaving town.
