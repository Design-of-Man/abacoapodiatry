# Copy-paste prompts, one block each

42 images, one per page. Copilot generates one image per turn, so this is one
block per message: copy a block, paste it, download the result, **save it as the filename
in the heading**, move to the next.

The style rules are already merged into every block below. Nothing to append, nothing to
assemble. Upload finished files to `assets/img/atmos/`.

**Regenerate if you see:** more or fewer than five toes, toes merging together, an arch
bending the wrong way, an ankle with no joint, anyone above the knee, or anything that
reads as a clinic. Shod feet fail far less often than bare ones.

**If you are short on time**, do *Conditions* and *Services* first. Those are the pages
patients reach from a search; the location pages are mostly local-SEO surface.
