What we treat in the Gardens

Gardens residents have two ways to reach us: this office right on Prosperity Farms Road, or the Jupiter laser suite on Military Trail. Same physician, same standards, whichever is closer.

Every treatment plan is designed by Dr. Orlando Cedeno, DPM, a board-certified foot and ankle surgeon — which means an honest read on every option from stretching to surgery, and advanced treatment only when it genuinely fits your case.
