Website Privacy Policy

Plain-language answers about the information this website collects and how it's used.

Information you send us

If you submit our appointment request form, the details you provide (name, phone, email, and your message) are used solely to contact you about scheduling and your care. Please do not submit detailed medical information through the website, that conversation belongs in a private clinical setting.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and any analytics tools we use may log standard technical data such as pages visited, browser type, and approximate location, used in aggregate to understand how the site is used and to improve it. The embedded Google Map on our contact page is served by Google and subject to Google's own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not share form submissions with third parties except the service providers needed to deliver them to our office.
  • This website does not collect or store your medical records. Patient health information is protected under our practice's HIPAA policies, available at our office.
