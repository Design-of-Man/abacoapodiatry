What we'll cover

Leg veins

Spider veins, varicose veins, and the heavy, achy, swollen-by-evening feeling that often comes with them. What the symptoms mean, when they're worth checking, and how sclerotherapy and vein ablation work.

Regenerative medicine

What regenerative medicine can do, and what it can't, for sore joints, stubborn tendons, and wounds that are slow to heal. We'll be straight about which uses have good evidence behind them and which are still early.

Peptides

What peptides are, why people are asking about them, and who they may or may not be appropriate for. This part is education only: no products, no prescriptions, and no promises.
