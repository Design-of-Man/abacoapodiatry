What is stem cell therapy?

Stem cell therapy is a regenerative treatment that places biologically active cells and growth factors directly into injured tendons, ligaments, or joints to support genuine tissue repair. Think of stem cells as the body's building blocks, unspecialized cells capable of supporting the repair and renewal of many tissue types.

When you're young and healthy, injuries recruit these repair resources readily. With age, chronic degeneration, or repeated injury, the supply lines run thin, which is one reason a 55-year-old's plantar fascia or Achilles tendon heals so much more slowly than a teenager's. Regenerative injections are designed to re-supply the injured area with concentrated biologic material, giving stalled healing a meaningful push.

At Jupiter Laser & Regenerative Medicine, regenerative treatment is never sold as a miracle. It's offered as part of an honest, physician-led plan: Dr. Cedeno evaluates your condition, explains what the evidence supports for your specific diagnosis, and tells you plainly whether cell therapy, PRP, laser, shockwave, or surgery, is the right tool.

What regenerative medicine can, and can't, do

Regenerative medicine is one of the most exciting areas in musculoskeletal care, and it's also one of the most over-marketed. Here's our commitment to you:

  • We'll tell you what the evidence shows for your specific diagnosis, including where research is strong, promising, or still emerging.
  • We'll never pressure you toward a cash-pay procedure you don't need.
  • We'll give you every option (conservative, regenerative, and surgical) with honest pros and cons, because Dr. Cedeno performs all three.
  • We'll measure results. Pain scores and function are tracked so you know whether treatment is working.

Regenerative therapies are not FDA-approved to treat, cure, or prevent any disease; individual results vary. A consultation is required to determine whether you're a candidate.
