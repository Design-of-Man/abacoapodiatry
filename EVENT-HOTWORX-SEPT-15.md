### Notes on this plan

**Captions carry no URL, by choice.** Instagram captions can't hold a live link
anyway, and the Google Business posts use a *Call now* button, which matches
sign-ups going to the office line. Facebook's first comment is the place to drop
`jupiterlaser.com/hotworx` if you want the link in the thread.

**Post Bridge publishes a standard Google Business update, not a native Event
post.** It does not expose the start/end date fields, so these will not carry the
event date badge. The true Event post — with the date range — still has to be
created by hand in the Google Business dashboard using the copy in section 2.
Worth doing both.

**TikTok is connected** (`abacoapodiatry`) but is deliberately left out. A static
poster makes a weak TikTok post; that one is worth waiting for the video Rose
offered to send.

**Reminder graphics** are generated by `_src/event_hotworx_reminders.py` at
1080×1350 and live in `assets/img/events/`. Reposting the poster four times in
four days reads as spam, so each reminder has its own card.
