What we operate on

  • Bunion correction, from tissue and partial-bone procedures to full forefoot realignment and joint fusion
  • Hammertoe and forefoot deformity repair
  • Flatfoot reconstruction, tendon repair, transfers, and bone realignment for collapsed arches
  • Tendon surgery, Achilles and posterior tibial repair and reconstruction
  • Fracture & trauma surgery, Dr. Cedeno trained specifically in reconstructive and trauma surgery of the foot and ankle
  • Joint procedures, from cheilectomy for stiff big toes to fusion for end-stage arthritis
  • Diabetic limb preservation, the surgical side of our wound care program

The minimally invasive difference

Dr. Mustafa has focused extensively on minimally invasive surgery (MIS), correcting bone and soft tissue through incisions of a few millimeters using specialized instruments under imaging guidance. For appropriate cases, that can mean less soft-tissue disruption, reduced postoperative discomfort, minimal scarring, and a faster return to shoes and life.

And because this is also a regenerative medicine practice, your recovery gets the full toolkit: MLS laser therapy to manage swelling and support tissue repair after surgery, with structured rehab to protect the result.

Why patients trust our OR decisions

  • Two surgeons, complementary strengths, reconstruction & trauma (Dr. Cedeno, FACFAS) and MIS & regenerative techniques (Dr. Mustafa)
  • You'll understand the plan, procedure, recovery timeline, and alternatives, before you decide
